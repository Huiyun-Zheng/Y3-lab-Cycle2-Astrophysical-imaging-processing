Neighbour-light model: focused mask and blending review

Run the existing source_detection_neighbour_model.py entry point.
Results overwrite this folder's results directory, as before.
The pre-change code and results are preserved in before_mask_blend_review.zip.
Root detection inputs and circular_moffat_analysis are not modified.

Changes in this version
1. Apply a right-edge strip mask at x >= 2450 and the previously located
   cross-shaped defect near (1036, 435), with a four-pixel safety margin.
   BADMASK, DETMASK, ARTEFACT and BOUNDARY are updated in the output FITS;
   STRIPMASK and MANUALMASK record the two additional exclusions separately.
2. Check every aperture for another detected peak/core and appreciable shared
   light. Both members of a significantly overlapping pair are excluded.
   This check applies to old usable sources AND model-recovered sources.
3. Check for strong internal multiple peaks that persist at two smoothing
   scales. This is a photometry-quality flag, not proof of two galaxies;
   genuine structured galaxies can also be flagged.
4. A segment being near a mask is no longer sufficient on its own to veto
   recovery. The actual aperture must avoid DETMASK and pass all image checks,
   then pass the existing two-initialisation neighbour-model checks.

5. Allow a bounded/unconstrained faint neighbour to contribute at most 3%
   of the target aperture flux (previously 1%). This provisional tolerance
   remains subject to the other fit residual, sky, stability and image cuts.
   It does not mean the contaminating flux is known to 3% accuracy.

Deliberately fixed
The candidate list, source coordinates, apertures, detection threshold,
background and RMS remain those in output/source_detection_results.
This script is a review of those detections; it does not rerun peak finding.
Candidates inside the new mask remain in the audit catalogue as excluded.
No aperture enlargement, neighbour subtraction, or target-slope tuning is used.
The existing model contamination limits remain 0.10 for area and flux.

New catalogue columns
mask_rejected: actual aperture touches the quality mask or image boundary.
neighbour_core_rejected: another detected peak/core enters this aperture.
shared_light_rejected: both apertures share significant measured signal,
  above 2% of each aperture's positive flux by default.
internal_multiple_peaks: a strong internal secondary peak is resolved.
image_quality_rejected: any of these image checks failed.
original_flags: original detection flags for comparison.
flags: current reasons, including successful model recovery when applicable.

At zero model-contamination limits, the baseline is now the original strict
sample AFTER these mask/image/SNR vetoes, not the historical usable count.
Increasing model limits cannot override a mask or a bright-core/shared-light veto.
A version-3 scored catalogue must be rescored; its old decisions cannot be reused.

Downstream photometry
Use results/sources.ecsv and results/detection_products.fits together.
Re-run the existing circular_moffat_analysis afterwards to regenerate counts
and effective area. Its old plot is not a result of this updated catalogue.
Remaining limitations: finite-aperture fluxes, empirical neighbour profiles,
uncertain classifications, and no completeness or cosmic-variance correction.
