Review after the excessive loss of faint detections
=================================================

Do not use the older 7-sigma masked_review as the default result.

It keeps the saved balanced catalogue, apertures, fluxes and background maps.
Only the right-strip and sharp-feature masks are added. Outputs overwrite
results/mask_only_control. It needs the saved masked_review mask products.

The aperture experiment is run_aperture_review.py.
It restores detection_sigma=5, peak_prominence_fraction=0.10 and aperture S/N=5.
It reruns the copied detector and neighbour fits, rather than altering old flags.
Outputs overwrite results/aperture_review.

For high-S/N sources, a larger measurement region provides a signed first radial
moment. Use a Kron-like scale of 2.5 and a minimum dimensionless radius of 1.0.
Keep the original shape/centre; mask neighbouring labels when estimating size.
Require >=90% clean coverage and background S/N >=50. Other sources keep their
original apertures. The 60-pixel size cap is retained.
The former minimum of 1.4 was removed because it enlarged apertures even when
the measured radial moment did not support that size. The added annulus must
also have >=90% clean coverage and signed flux / background noise >=3.
This noise gate is a size diagnostic, not a calibrated photometric uncertainty.
The first-moment approach follows the idea documented at:
https://photutils.readthedocs.io/en/stable/api/photutils.segmentation.SourceCatalog.html
This is a custom implementation, not an invocation of Photutils SourceCatalog.

Recheck the larger apertures using the neighbour model and all hard mask checks.
If an enlarged aperture fails, remeasure and recheck the original smaller one.
Accept the smaller one only if it passes. Record aperture_wings_limited=True and
aperture_method=original_clean_fallback. Do not claim that its flux is total flux.

Every final aperture, including strict sources and fallbacks, is now checked for
neighbour-core intrusion. Identify other-label pixels above 30% of that label's
peak, where the neighbour peak is >=10 local RMS. Reject an aperture containing
>=2 such pixels that together supply >=1% of its positive observed flux.
These are provisional quality thresholds, not a true contaminating-flux model.
An overlap between drawn ellipse outlines alone is not a rejection criterion.
The original joint-model contamination checks still apply.

A manually identified cross-shaped defect at zero-based (1036,432), previously
ID 44, is excluded before source detection. Two narrow rectangular arms are
recorded in results/aperture_review/manual_exclusions.csv and MANUALMASK in the
products FITS. BADMASK, ARTEFACT and DETMASK include this exclusion consistently.
Its physical origin is not established. Nearby sources at (1048,472) and
(1028,403) remain usable. IDs change on redetection; use positions for comparison.

quality_changes.csv records positional matches and before/after measurements.
Run audit_aperture_quality.py to regenerate these comparisons after a full run.
The larger apertures and recovered bright objects change the measured magnitudes
and counts. They do not guarantee a steeper count slope. This experiment is kept
separate; it is NOT adopted merely because its circles cover more visible light.

No fit interval was changed to force the slope above 0.4 or toward 0.6.
The mask-only result is the controlled baseline. The aperture experiment needs
further completeness/photometric-bias validation before replacing that baseline.
Neither remaining sample is a catalogue of individually confirmed galaxies.
