Purpose
Review all 7038 existing candidates for missed internal peaks and reconsider
excluded bright objects using their actual aperture measurements. This is a
provisional quality review, not a completeness correction or a trained physical
star/galaxy/blend classifier. The cuts were not selected to obtain a target slope.

Internal peaks
The old neighbour checks can miss two peaks inside one segmentation label.
We examine each fixed aperture at Gaussian smoothing widths 0.5 and 0.7 pixels.
Peaks must belong to the source label, recur at both scales, and initially exceed
five times the unsmoothed local RMS. Connected flat maxima are counted once.
For each source the secondary with the largest saddle depth is recorded.
The provisional rejection requires all of:
  separation >=5 pixels (median stellar FWHM is approximately 4 pixels);
  secondary peak / local unsmoothed RMS >=7.5;
  secondary / primary peak >=0.15;
  secondary-to-saddle drop / local RMS >=1.5;
  positive flux in a 2-pixel secondary core >=2% of positive aperture flux.
These are descriptive quality tolerances, not calibrated false-alarm thresholds.
Real galaxies can have multiple knots; this does not establish two galaxies.
It removes the suspect single-aperture measurement, without counting it twice.
62 old usable objects have recurring peaks; 9 meet every rejection condition.
The other 53 remain usable with a review warning.

Bright recovery
465 originally excluded objects have measured aperture r<22. All are audited.
94 pass the preliminary gates and receive a longer, three-start neighbour fit.
Recovery requires a wholly clean measurement aperture, no neighbour-core or
neighbour-peak intrusion, no boundary/cap failure, aperture S/N>=50, and the
existing model quality cuts. Modelled neighbour flux must be <=3% and its
between-start disagreement <=1%. For shared-light rejections, the target's
observed shared fraction must be <=2%, with all rejected partners excluded.
The shared fraction is NOT itself the true neighbour contamination fraction.
16 objects pass. 11 originally failed shared-aperture checks after passing their
model, and 5 carried segmentation-wide near-artefact flags. Historical flags
are preserved alongside field_review_recovered and field_model_* audit columns.
No model light is subtracted; adopted fluxes are signed sums in fixed apertures.
Existing finite aperture fluxes are preserved exactly. Missing fluxes for newly
accepted objects are filled from the actual pixels, not the fitted profile.

Warnings retained in the nominal sample
review_queue.ecsv contains 233 usable objects needing further review:
  53 weaker recurring internal peaks;
  153 narrow or elongated moment shapes;
  26 very bright compact apertures with outer-light/coverage concerns;
  1 screenshot source (2099) with a secondary feature surviving only one scale.
Shape alone is not enough to remove an edge-on galaxy or a noisy faint source.
The compact-core check examines circular apertures of radius<12 pixels with
peak_detection_snr>1000. It checks additional light out to 1.4 times the radius.
This is a targeted warning, not a full curve-of-growth test for every aperture.
It blocks new recoveries but does not remove existing usable sources. In
particular, IDs 3 and 20 remain excluded because their small cores need further
aperture work. Detection thresholds and faint-source selection are unchanged.

Results
Usable: 5669 -> 5676 (9 removed, 16 recovered).
Likely stars: 69. Remaining sample: 5607 (not all confirmed galaxies).
Same PSF reference positions and 19--23 magnitude fit interval.
Descriptive cumulative-count slope: 0.377414 +/-0.012454.
The baseline slope was approximately 0.390711. A greater slope is not used as
the acceptance criterion: recovered bright objects can flatten cumulative counts.
The original baseline remains available for comparison.

Validation
Run validate_field_quality_review.py after the analysis. It checks synthetic
single/elongated/double-source controls, baseline preservation, fixed geometry,
flux sums, recovery masks and metrics, shared pairs, and the photometry sample.
It regenerates the review queue and review plots. Expected result counts are
regression checks for this particular saved baseline, not general constants.
See results/field_quality_review/VALIDATION.txt for the completed checks.
Automated checks cover all candidates; visual review covers flagged cutouts
and the full-field overview. This does not certify every retained source as clean.
