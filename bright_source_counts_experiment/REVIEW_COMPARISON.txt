The aperture experiment is run_aperture_review.py.
It restores detection_sigma=5, peak_prominence_fraction=0.10 and aperture S/N=5.
It reruns the copied detector and neighbour fits, rather than altering old flags.
Outputs overwrite results/aperture_review.

For high-S/N sources, a larger measurement region provides a signed first radial
moment. Use a Kron-like scale of 2.5 and a minimum dimensionless radius of 1.0.
Keep the original shape/centre; mask neighbouring labels when estimating size.
Require >=90% clean coverage and background S/N >=50. Other sources keep their
original apertures. The 60-pixel size cap is retained.
The former minimum of 1.4 was removed because it enlarged apertures even when
the measured radial moment did not support that size. The added annulus must
also have >=90% clean coverage and signed flux / background noise >=3.
This noise gate is a size diagnostic, not a calibrated photometric uncertainty.
The first-moment approach follows the idea documented at:
https://photutils.readthedocs.io/en/stable/api/photutils.segmentation.SourceCatalog.html
This is a custom implementation, not an invocation of Photutils SourceCatalog.

Recheck the larger apertures using the neighbour model and all hard mask checks.
If an enlarged aperture fails, remeasure and recheck the original smaller one.
Accept the smaller one only if it passes. Record aperture_wings_limited=True and
aperture_method=original_clean_fallback. Do not claim that its flux is total flux.

Every final aperture, including strict sources and fallbacks, is now checked for
neighbour-core intrusion. Identify other-label pixels above 30% of that label's
peak, where the neighbour peak is >=10 local RMS. Reject an aperture containing
>=2 such pixels that together supply >=1% of its positive observed flux.
These are provisional quality thresholds, not a true contaminating-flux model.
An overlap between drawn ellipse outlines alone is not a rejection criterion.
The original joint-model contamination checks still apply.

A further symmetric shared-aperture check addresses overlapping outer wings,
which may be below 30% of a neighbour's peak. It uses actual shared pixels, not
drawn ellipse boundaries. Reject BOTH members of a pair if at least 3 shared
pixels exceed 3 local RMS, the shared signed flux has background S/N >=5, and
that flux is >=0.5% of the positive aperture flux of EACH member. Other excluded
sources still count as possible neighbours. This is a conservative independent-
aperture sample definition, not a calibrated true-contamination fraction or a
claim that blended sources could never be measured with dedicated deblending.
SHARED_FLUX_LIMIT in run_aperture_review.py sets the shared-light tolerance.

The preceding joint fits sometimes assigned tiny faint neighbours extremely
broad Moffat profiles (alpha at the old 45-pixel upper bound, sometimes beta at
1.2), with compensating background offsets and different contamination scores
from the two starting points. Bright nearby sources then failed stability cuts.
run_aperture_review.py now imports aperture_neighbour_model.py, a copy of the
previous model. It fits log FWHM axes directly rather than log alpha axes.
Each FWHM upper bound is max(6 pixels, 3 times the measured moment sigma on that
axis). The six-pixel floor allows for faint, truncated detection segments.
These bounds are empirical regularisation, not a calibrated PSF measurement.
The beta derivative includes its effect on alpha at fixed FWHM. Finite-difference
checks, half-maximum checks and synthetic noiseless recovery pass.
All contamination, sky, residual, bound-sensitivity and shared-light limits are
unchanged. Bound-limited neighbours still cause rejection if their predicted
contribution is too large. Flux remains the measured aperture sum, not a
substituted model flux. The smaller-aperture fallback is remeasured and refitted.

reviewed_wings.py explicitly selects only these two reviewed positions. It
requires an already usable, strict, unblended circle with aperture S/N >=500.
A clean reference circle extends to 2.5 times the pre-review radius. All reference
pixels must be finite, unmasked and free of other segmentation labels. Use
signed flux, not clipped-positive flux. Require >3% extra flux, wing S/N >=10,
and <2% of reference flux in the outermost 20% of its radius. Choose the first
radius (0.25-pixel steps) enclosing 98% of the finite reference-circle flux;
require >3-sigma wing light in at least six of eight angular sectors.
This is a local empirical correction, not an automatic field-wide rule or a
claim to measure 98% of total light. Neighbour-core and shared-pixel rejection
still run afterwards. A repeated run uses the saved pre-review radius, so the
apertures do not keep growing on successive executions.
